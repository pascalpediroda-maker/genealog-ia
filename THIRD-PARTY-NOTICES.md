## sliday/genealogy-research

**What is used.** The catalogue of archive holdings outside France — national archives,
parish databases, war-dead registers and migration indexes, from Poland to Argentina —
carried here as `skills/archives-fr/references/monde.md`. Their catalogue was reorganised
and annotated; the substance of the listing is theirs.

**Where it comes from.** <https://github.com/sliday/genealogy-research>
